4 · Return
Closing the window, or Move to Board in its ⋯ menu, slides the tile back into its original slot with a one-time accent halo (600 ms). Selection stays where you were; no focus is stolen.
Why an explicit "Move to Board". Closing a window to bring a project back feels like it might stop things. A named action in the project's ⋯ menu (and Window menu) says what happens. Closing still works the same.
First paint after relaunch. No animation. Extracted projects are simply absent; the foot line is already there; the board selects the first remaining project, or shows "The board is clear".
