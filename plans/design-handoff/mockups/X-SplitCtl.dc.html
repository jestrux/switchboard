LIMITS
320 × 180
≈ 40 × 8
A split is refused if either half would fall under 320 × 180 pt, or past 4 panes. The Split button dims with the reason as its tooltip; ⌘D shows the same reason as a toast. Dragging a divider stops at the minimum.
Making a split. ⌘D right, ⌘⇧D down, the split button in the bar (⌥-click for down), or the View menu. The new pane takes half of the focused one.
Focus. Click anywhere in a pane, ⌘[ and ⌘], or ⌘⌥ + arrows. Keystrokes go only to the pane with the accent rule; clicking a sidebar row targets that pane.
Closing. ✕ or ⌘⇧W. The neighbour takes the space and focus; the command keeps running. The last pane can't be closed; ⌘W still closes the window.
Memory. Each project remembers its split tree, ratios and which command sits where. Selecting the project on the board, or opening its window, restores it. View › Reset Layout returns to one pane.
